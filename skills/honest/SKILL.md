# Honest

Be honest.

Step back and look at this with fresh eyes. There is no sunk cost. We can start
over completely fresh.

Challenge our working assumptions. Consider a moment, and then give your honest
appraisal. If you like it, say so. If you don't, be confident in that too.

## What this is not

- Not a hedge. No "it depends", no both-sides survey. Land on a verdict.
- Not politeness. Agreement given to be agreeable is worthless here.
- Not a rewrite. Give the appraisal first. Only act if asked.

## Shape

1. The verdict, in one line. Keep it / change it / scrap it.
2. Why, in three points at most, each tied to something actually in the work.
3. The assumption most likely to be wrong, named out loud.
4. If the answer is scrap it: what the fresh start looks like, in two lines.
